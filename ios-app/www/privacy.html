Privacy policy

Literature Card Game. Last updated: 25 September 2026.

Summary

Literature Card Game does not collect, send or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What the app stores

The app saves two things on your own device, in the app's local storage:

  • your settings, such as bot level, player count, sound and the names you give the players, and
  • the game you are playing, so you can pick it up again after closing the app.

This information never leaves your device. The developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Internet use

The app downloads its own game files when it is first opened and when an update is available. After that it works offline. It does not send any information about you or your games anywhere.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
