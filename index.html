    Coach

      Each move is tested by playing out hundreds of imagined deals that fit what's been said at the table. Percentages are estimates.

      Notes

      Based only on your hand and what's been said at the table. “?” means three or more players could hold it; a coloured edge means they're all on one team. Hover a card for the full list.

      How to play
      • Books: each suit splits into a low book (2–7) and a high book (9–A). All four 8s and both jokers make a ninth book.
      • Ask one opponent for one card from a book you already hold a card in. If they have it, they must hand it over and you go again. If not, it becomes their turn.
      • Bluff by asking for a card you already hold. It always misses, but it can mislead the table.
      • Call a book on your turn when you think you and June hold all six cards between you. If you're right, your team gets the book. If you're wrong, it goes to Basil and Otto.
      • Out: a player with no cards is out, and their partner carries on alone.
      • The first team to 5 books wins.
